\section{Evidence and method}\label{sec:evidence}

Explain what the evidence can establish, how it was selected, and where its
limits sit. Separate observation from inference.

\subsection{Analytical sequence}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item Establish the relevant baseline.
  \item Test the central mechanism against the strongest evidence.
  \item Examine the most credible alternative explanation.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{sidenote}
\textbf{Evidence discipline.} Add only sources you have opened and verified.
Do not leave a citation carrying a claim broader than the cited material.
\end{sidenote}
